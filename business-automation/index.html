Signs your business is ready for automation

  • You copy the same details into more than one place.
  • Orders or enquiries arrive in several places — website, DMs, email, phone.
  • Customers wait for replies on busy days.
  • End-of-week reports take hours to put together.
  • You can't take on more work without hiring.
