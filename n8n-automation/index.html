Questions05
What is n8n?

A workflow automation tool. You connect apps — like Gmail, Google Sheets or your payment provider — and n8n runs the steps between them on a trigger or a schedule, including AI steps.

Should I use n8n or Zapier?

Zapier is quick for simple, two-step jobs. n8n suits longer workflows with logic, AI agents and approvals, and it can be self-hosted. I'll recommend whichever fits your job.

Can you fix or extend an existing n8n workflow?

Yes — send me what you have and what you'd like it to do.

Do I need my own n8n account?

Not necessarily. It can run on your account or be set up and hosted for you.

How much does an n8n build cost?

Every project gets a fixed price after a free 15-minute call, agreed before any work starts.
