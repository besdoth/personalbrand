What AI automation actually does for a small business

Not a chatbot bolted onto your website. AI automation connects the tools you already use — your inbox, your forms, your spreadsheets, your payments — and lets AI do the reading, sorting and writing in between.

01

Answers customer emails

Every new email is read, sorted by what the customer needs, and answered using your own policies and their account details.

02

Sorts enquiries and leads

Website forms, DMs and emails are read, classified and routed, so the ones that matter reach you first.

03

Keeps you in control

Anything involving money or judgement — a refund, a quote, an exception — is sent to your phone for a yes or no before it happens.

04

Writes the boring bits

Follow-ups, reminders and summaries drafted in your tone and sent at the right time.

Questions05
How much does AI automation cost?

It depends on what's involved, so every project gets a fixed price after a free 15-minute call. You agree the price before any work starts.

Will AI reply to my customers without me checking?

Only where you're comfortable with it. Routine questions can be answered automatically; anything involving money or a judgement call — like a refund — can wait for your approval on your phone first.

What tools does it work with?

Most things with an inbox or an API: Gmail and Outlook, Google Sheets, website forms, payment providers, Telegram and more. I build in n8n, which connects to hundreds of apps.

What can the automation see and do?

Only what you connect it to. Before anything is built we go through what data is involved, so you know exactly what each automation can see and do.

Do you only work with businesses in Leeds?

No — I work with businesses across the UK, remotely. Some of my clients are in Leeds.
