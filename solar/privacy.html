Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Who we are

BrightHome Energy ("we", "us") provides solar panel and air source heat pump installations in the UK. You can contact us at hello@example.co.uk or on 0800 000 0000.

What we collect and why

When you submit our quote request form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, postcode, what you're interested in, and any message you choose to add. We use this information for one purpose: to contact you about your quotation and, if you go ahead, to arrange your survey and installation.

How your data is handled

Form submissions are delivered to us by Web3Forms (a form delivery service) and arrive as an email to our inbox. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. If you take out finance, we will share the details you provide with the finance provider you choose — we'll always tell you before we do.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your quotation, and no longer than 24 months if you don't become a customer.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies itself. If you arrived via an advert, the advertising platform (e.g. Google) may use its own cookies subject to its own policies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email hello@example.co.uk and we'll action your request. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
