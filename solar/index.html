Why make the switch?

Solar and heat pumps aren't just greener — they can seriously cut what you pay every month.

Lower energy bills

Generate your own electricity and heat your home more efficiently — many households cut their bills significantly from day one.

Get paid to export

With the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG), your energy supplier pays you for the spare electricity your panels send back to the grid.

The government's Boiler Upgrade Scheme offers eligible homes £7,500 off the cost of an air source heat pump installation.

Solar panels and heat pumps currently qualify for 0% VAT in the UK — meaning more of your money goes into your system.

Frequently asked questions

How much do solar panels or a heat pump cost?

It depends on your home's size, roof and energy usage. A typical solar system starts from a few thousand pounds, and heat pumps can be reduced by up to £7,500 with the Boiler Upgrade Scheme. Your free quotation will give you exact figures — with monthly finance options if you'd rather spread the cost.

Is my roof suitable for solar panels?

Most UK roofs are. South, east or west-facing roofs all work well, and our free survey checks pitch, shading and structure before we recommend anything.

Do heat pumps work in older UK homes?

Yes — modern air source heat pumps work in a wide range of properties, including older homes. Our survey checks your insulation and radiators to make sure a heat pump will keep your home warm efficiently.

What grants and incentives can I get?

Eligible households can get £7,500 towards a heat pump through the Boiler Upgrade Scheme, 0% VAT applies to installations, and the Smart Export Guarantee pays you for electricity your solar panels export. We handle the paperwork for you.

What happens after I send the form?

We'll call you back — usually within one working day — for a friendly, no-pressure chat. If you'd like to go further, we arrange a free home survey and put together your personalised quotation.
